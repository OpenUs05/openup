Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026 · Applies to the OpenUS app on the web, iPhone and Android.

The short version. OpenUS only shares what you choose to share, only with the person you connect with. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share anything secretly. You can stop sharing, pause, or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

OpenUS ("we", "us") is a consent-based app for couples and families. Questions about this policy can be sent through the form on our support page.

OpenUS does not require an email address or phone number, and does not collect contacts from your phone, advertising IDs or analytics profiles.

Location

  • Location is collected only after you turn on Share my location and allow location access on your phone.
  • On the web version, location updates only while OpenUS is open. In the iPhone and Android apps, if you allow it, OpenUS may also use your location when the app is closed or not in use, only to share it with the person you connected with and to send safe-place arrive/leave alerts.
  • Your last known location is shown to your partner for up to 2 days. Arrive/leave events are kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Location is never used for ads and never sold or shared with anyone else.

Consent and control

  • Nothing is shared until both people set up OpenUS on their own phones and connect with a sync code.
  • Each person chooses what they share. Either person can pause the connection or turn sharing off at any time.
  • OpenUS is not designed to be used secretly. Do not install or use OpenUS on someone else's phone without their knowledge and agreement.

Children

OpenUS is not directed at children under 13. In Family mode, a parent or guardian sets up and manages alerts for their own child. If you believe a child's data was added without a guardian's consent, contact us through the support page and we'll delete it.

Where data is kept and how it's protected

Synced data is stored with our hosting provider, Netlify, on servers in the United States. All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Each phone gets its own private access key; anyone without it can't read your synced space. Some data is also saved on your own phone so the app works offline.

Who we share data with

Only the person you connect with, plus the service providers that run OpenUS: Netlify (hosting and storage) and your phone's notification service (Apple, Google or your browser's push service) to deliver alerts. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

How long we keep it

Synced data is kept until you delete it. Arrive/leave history is deleted after 30 days. If a synced space isn't used for 12 months, we may delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing: turn off Share my location, or pause the connection, in the app.
  • Stop syncing a phone: Your connection → Stop syncing on this phone.
  • Delete everything: on our support page, delete your synced space permanently, or ask us to.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us through the support page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
