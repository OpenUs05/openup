Common questions

How do I connect two phones?

On the first phone, open Your connection and tap Create sync code. On the second phone, enter that code on the first screen and tap Join, then pick who you are.

Location isn't updating.

Make sure Share my location is on and your phone allows location for OpenUS. The web version only updates while OpenUS is open on screen.

I'm not getting notifications.

On the Location screen, under Live GPS, tap Turn on for notifications. On iPhone, add OpenUS to your home screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Delete my data

This permanently deletes the synced OpenUS space for you and the person you're connected with: profiles, photos, boundaries, check-ins, contacts, safe places, locations and history. It can't be undone. OpenUS data saved on each phone stays until you tap Start over in the app.

Open this page on a phone that's synced with OpenUS. Or use the contact form above and pick "Delete my data".
